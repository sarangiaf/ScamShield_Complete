Indian language coverage

Listing a language does not mean full scam detection support.

Coverage limitation: Starter phrase rules are included for English, Hindi, Hinglish, Bengali, Gujarati, Marathi, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Assamese and Urdu. Other languages are listed but do not have dedicated phrase dictionaries in this version. This is a rule-based prototype, not a trained AI model. It can miss scams phrased differently.

How ScamTrace works

Simple, explainable local analysis.

🔎

Phrase matching

Looks for configured words linked to OTPs, account blocking, payments, prizes and urgency.

🧮

Risk scoring

Combines matching signals into a heuristic score. It is not a probability that a scam is real.

🔐

Local by design

Message analysis happens in the browser without an external analysis API.

Safety checklist

Steps to take when a message seems suspicious.

✓
01

Never share an OTP, UPI PIN, password or full card details in response to an unsolicited message.

02

Do not open unexpected links or install apps/APK files sent by unknown contacts.

03

Verify claims using an official app or website that you open yourself.

04

If money has been lost to cyber fraud in India, contact your bank immediately and call the cyber-fraud helpline 1930.
