8 tips

📩

Beware of Suspicious Messages

Unexpected prizes, urgent payments or threats may be scam attempts.

💡 Verify the sender independently.
🔒

Never Share Your OTP

Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords or banking credentials.

💡 A UPI PIN is not needed to receive money.
🔑

Use Strong Passwords

Use long, unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication.

💡 Never reuse one password everywhere.
⚙️

Keep Devices Updated

Update your phone, browser, computer and apps regularly.

💡 Turn on automatic security updates.
📶

Be Careful on Public Wi-Fi

Avoid sensitive banking on untrusted public networks.

💡 Mobile data is a safer choice for sensitive transactions.
🔗

Check Links Before Clicking

Look for misspelled domains and suspicious shortened links.

💡 Visit official websites directly.
🛡️

Use Trusted Security Tools

Keep device protections enabled and use trusted security tools.

💡 No detector catches every scam.
🚨

Report Online Fraud

For financial cyber fraud in India, contact your bank quickly.

💡 Report at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
