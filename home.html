      03 / LEARN
      01📨

      Social Engineering

      A fake authority, urgent threat or tempting reward pushes someone to act quickly.

      02🎭

      Impersonation

      A message or page pretends to represent a bank, delivery service or known brand.

      03🔑

      Information Request

      The page may ask for passwords, OTPs, payment details or other sensitive information.

      04🛡️

      Defensive Action

      Stop, open the official app yourself, verify the claim and report suspicious activity.
